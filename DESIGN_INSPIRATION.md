# Moayed Cafe Directory — Design Inspiration

Locked animation direction: **Cinematic scroll + Playful kinetic** hybrid.

## Palette

- Espresso: `#2C1810`
- Cream: `#F5F0E8`
- Copper accent: `#B87333`
- Tan: `#D4A574`
- Sage: `#8A9A7B`
- Gold (top scores): `#C9A227`

## Typography

- Display: **Fraunces** (serif, warm)
- Body: **DM Sans**
- Arabic support: **Noto Sans Arabic**

## Cinematic scroll (kept)

- Lenis smooth scroll globally
- GSAP ScrollTrigger: map scales down slightly as leaderboard enters
- Leaderboard rows: `y: 80 → 0`, opacity, stagger 0.08s
- Full Data reveal: scale-up with cinematic ease
- Category switch: crossfade + staggered card entrance

## Playful kinetic (kept)

- Map pins: spring bounce on load, pulse when selected
- Score numbers: CountUp on viewport enter
- Full Data button: magnetic hover + ripple
- Category buttons: copper glow + scale on active
- Coffee brown and tan tones throughout

## Removed

- Editorial luxury direction (oversized minimal editorial layout)
